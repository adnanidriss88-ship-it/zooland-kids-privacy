Zooland Kids - Data Deletion

Developer / Publisher: Adnan Idriss

Zooland Kids does not provide online user accounts. Child profiles, learning progress, rewards, game history, and related app data are stored locally on the user's device.

Delete data stored on the device

Inside Zooland Kids, use New profile to remove the current child's local profile, rewards, English progress, game progress, and child-specific cached data. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app also removes locally stored app data from that device.

Request deletion

To request deletion of any Zooland Kids data that may be under the developer's control, email:

adnan.idriss88@gmail.com

Please use the subject Zooland Kids Data Deletion Request and include enough information for us to understand your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

Zooland Kids uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for advertising and privacy requirements. Data processed directly by Google is handled under Google's own privacy and retention practices. For information about Google's privacy practices, visit Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about privacy or data deletion for Zooland Kids, contact adnan.idriss88@gmail.com.
