Zooland Kids Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Zooland Kids is a child-directed educational app for ages 3–12, developed and published by Adnan Idriss in Lebanon. Child profile and learning progress are stored locally on the device. The current app build does not operate its own account or analytics server.

Information kept on this device

A parent or child can create a local profile containing a display name, age, avatar, learning progress, game history, rewards, streaks and app settings. These items are stored in the app's local device storage so the experience can continue between sessions.

Children's privacy

Zooland Kids is designed for children ages 3–12. This build has no developer-operated sign-in service, social features, chat, location tracking, or analytics backend. We do not sell child profile data.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, such as watching an ad to earn one hint. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment, a maximum G content rating, and non-personalized ads. The Android Advertising ID permission is removed from the app manifest.

Google may still process technical information needed to deliver, secure, limit and report ads under Google's own privacy terms.

Privacy choices for ads

Before ads may be requested, Zooland Kids asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for the current privacy requirements and marks the user as under the age of consent. The app does not ask a child to provide adult consent.

If Google requires a privacy-options entry point for the device or region, it is available from the in-app Privacy & Ads screen.

Device speech features

Educational words may be spoken using the device's text-to-speech service. The operating system or speech provider may handle that request according to the device owner's settings and the provider's own terms.

Data security

Zooland Kids keeps the child profile, learning progress, game history and rewards in the app's local device storage and does not send that local profile data to a developer-operated server. Access to local app data is controlled by the device operating system and app sandbox.

Third-party services used by the app apply their own security practices to information they process.

Data retention

Local child profile and progress data remain on the device until a parent or user resets progress, creates a new profile, clears the app's data, or uninstalls the app. App-level settings may remain until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Data processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' own policies.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for ad privacy requirements.
  • The device text-to-speech service for spoken educational content.

The current build does not use a developer analytics backend or ad mediation network.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Resetting or deleting local child data

Reset progress keeps the child's local profile and avatar but clears learning and game progress.

New profile removes the current child's local profile, rewards, English progress, game progress and child caches from Zooland Kids while preserving app-level settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the current app build. If Zooland Kids adds accounts, analytics, cloud storage, new advertising technology or other data uses, this policy and the app-store disclosures will be updated before those changes are released.
